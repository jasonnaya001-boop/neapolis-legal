# Privacy Policy for Neapolis

**Effective date:** [FILL IN DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how Neapolis (Jason Naya and his team) handles information when you use the app. Neapolis is a piano sight-reading practice app.

## 1. Who we are

Neapolis is developed by Jason Naya. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at jasonnaya@live.com.

## 2. Information we collect

**Microphone audio.** Neapolis uses your device's microphone to listen to your playing and check your sight-reading accuracy in real time. This audio is processed entirely **on your device** using an on-device pitch-detection model. We do not record, store, transmit, or otherwise have access to your microphone audio — it never leaves your device.

**Sheet music you import.**
- Browsing the built-in public-domain sheet library sends a request to our server to fetch the list of available scores and the score files themselves.
- If you import a PDF to convert it into playable sheet music, that PDF file is uploaded to our server, converted, and the result is returned to your device. We do not use uploaded files for any purpose other than performing that conversion, and do not claim ownership of anything you upload.

**App settings and preferences.** Things like your Pro subscription status, microphone calibration/sensitivity settings, and display preferences are stored **locally on your device** (not on our servers) and are removed if you uninstall the app.

**Purchase information.** Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play billing systems. We do not collect or have access to your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — Apple/Google handle that in accordance with their own privacy policies.

**Standard technical data.** Like any app or website that talks to a server, requests to our backend (e.g. fetching sheet music) include standard technical information such as your IP address and basic device/request metadata, which may appear in ordinary server logs. We do not use this to build a profile of you or track you across other apps or websites.

We do **not** currently require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other personal identifiers to use the app.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above only to:
- Provide core app functionality (sight-reading feedback, sheet music library, PDF import/conversion).
- Maintain and secure our servers.
- Diagnose technical problems.

## 4. How we share information

- **We do not sell your data.**
- **We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.**
- Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies:
  - [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
  - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public.

## 5. Data retention

- Microphone audio is never stored — it's processed in memory on your device and discarded.
- Locally stored settings (Pro status, calibration, preferences) remain on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- PDFs you upload for conversion are processed in a temporary location on our server and deleted immediately once the conversion finishes — we do not keep a copy.

## 6. Children's privacy

Neapolis is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at **jasonnaya@live.com** and we will delete it.

## 7. Your choices

- You can use almost all of the app's practice features without any data ever leaving your device (microphone processing is fully on-device).
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored settings.
- PDFs you upload for conversion are never retained on our server, so there's nothing to request deletion of.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information in transit to and from our servers (e.g. HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.
