# Neapolis Support

Need help with Neapolis, or have a question, bug report, or feature request?

Email us at **jasonnaya@live.com** and we'll get back to you.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Neapolis doesn't require sign-up — everything works right away.

**Does the microphone recording get sent anywhere?**
No. Microphone audio is processed entirely on your device for real-time pitch detection and is never recorded, stored, or uploaded.

**How do I restore a purchase on a new device?**
Open the Pro upgrade screen and tap "Restore purchases."

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, not inside the app.

See our [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for details on how Neapolis handles data.
